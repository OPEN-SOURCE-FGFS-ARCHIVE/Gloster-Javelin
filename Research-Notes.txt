Engine RPM Max 101.5
Engine RPM Min 35

Spool time from idle to full = 14 sec

SFC = 0.885
Engine weight = 3050lb

'Wee-Heat' is on or off, no variability, should be able to calculate ATSFC if not available from core RPM drop at low altitude (12%)

Standard Approach RPM min 60% - 75% with airbrakes

Stall Speeds:	90Kts Flaps down
		100Kts Flaps up

Standard Approach at 160Kts

Flaps up approach is 3 degrees higher nose high, standard glideslope (3 degrees), but tail strike is possible, so stick is pushed forward just before touchdown

Tail strike angle is ~8 degrees, so with ~3 degrees extra room for full gear compression, approach aoa is probably 6-8 degrees with flaps down (nose will be 3-5 degrees above horizon)

CG postion - 0.307 MAC

Tail plane is all-moving, elevator is anti-balance tab (moves same direction as tail plane rather than opposite), trim is via tailplane angle

Fuel Capacity:	915 gal / 7045 lb internal 
				500 gal / 3850 lb total in ventral tanks

				collector tanks are 40 gal / 308 lb (~15 sec inverted flight - but this is probably an oil limit)

				10 wing tanks (5 / side)
				 - tank 3 each side is at CG and holds 120 gal each
				 - tanks 1 & 2 are forward of CG
				 - tanks 4 & 5 are behind CG 
				 - ventral tanks empty first
				 - wing tanks empty evenly (same rate across all)
				 - tanks 3 can be isolated to act as reserve 
				 - all tanks are inboard of the guns
				 - 1 is at leading edge inboard
				 - 2 is between 1 & main gear bay inboard
				 - 3 is outboard of 1 & 2 at CG 
				 - 4 is behind main gear bay inboard
				 - 5 is behind main gear bay outboard
